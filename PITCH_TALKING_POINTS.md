### **[0:35–1:20] Slide 4: The Real Problem**
* **Passive DVRs:** Cameras only record footage *after* a tragedy happens.
* **Medical Emergencies:** Collapses and heart attacks on streets go unnoticed for minutes.

* **Morocco Digital 2030:** Need proactive safety with **$0 Capex**.

---

### **[1:20–2:05] Slide 5: The Solution (3 Pillars)**
* **NLP Search:** Type/speak natural descriptions *(“child in red cap, yellow shirt”)*—no forms.
* **Autonomous Fall Detection:** Instantly flags collapses with zero human latency.
* **Privacy by Design:** **CNDP Law 09-08 compliant**—no facial biometrics, tracks clothing/colors, auto-blurs faces.

---

### **[2:05–2:40] Slides 6–7: Architecture & Edge Sovereignty**
* **4 Clean Layers:** Perception (cameras) → Network (fiber) → Platform (Edge AI) → Application (Dashboard).
* **Speed:** Runs locally at **30+ FPS** on standard PC hardware.
* **Data Sovereignty:** 100% on-premises edge computing—zero cloud leaks, zero external server fees.
